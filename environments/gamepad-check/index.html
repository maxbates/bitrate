Connecting (works over plain HTTP — Mac or iPad, no HTTPS needed):
• Bluetooth: pair the controller in the OS — Xbox Series (hold ⧉ + pair button) or DualSense/DualShock (hold PS + Share) → System Settings ▸ Bluetooth / iPad Settings ▸ Bluetooth.
• USB: just plug it in.
• Then press any button here — browsers hide the gamepad until you do.
• Both sticks read as an 8-point direction (deadzone in the middle). Left × right = 8×8 = 64 combos ≈ 5.98 bits per two-stick event.
